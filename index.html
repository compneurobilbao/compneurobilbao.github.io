The meeting

Built for exchange, kept simple.

01

Who it is for

Biomedical engineering students from the Basque Country and nearby regions, alongside researchers, clinical professionals, engineers, technology centres and companies in the biomedical ecosystem.

02

Topics

Neurophysiological data, brain–body interactions and translation into clinical applications and medical devices.

  • Neurotechnology
  • Electromedicine
  • Multimodal neuroimaging
  • Physiological analysis
